# Workday Source of Truth and Synchronization

In the prototype, Employee Service and Leave Service own synthetic operational data. Approval Service owns local approval workflows and immutable manager-decision audit records. Leave Service owns leave-request lifecycle and business effects, including balance reservation, release, and consumption. Internal mock Workday adapters live within the Employee and Leave service boundaries.

When enterprise integration is enabled, Workday may become authoritative only for explicitly configured employee or absence records and processes. Typical candidate fields are worker ID, employment status, organization, manager, location/country, effective dates, leave plans, entitlements, balances, and absence requests. Workday does not automatically own every approval decision. The Approval Service continues to own its local workflow and immutable audit unless a future, explicitly approved process-level source-of-truth matrix assigns a defined external absence process to Workday.

Before any live integration, document a field/process-level source-of-truth matrix, synchronization direction, version/effective-date behavior, and conflict-resolution policy. Services retain local projections and local-only metadata. Inbound versioned, effective-dated Workday updates update only configured projections. Outbound commands carry stable correlation and idempotency IDs. For Workday-authoritative fields, accepted upstream versions win; local-only fields are preserved. Unresolvable conflicts enter an explicit integration-conflict state for HR Administrator review rather than being silently overwritten.
